How to Use Litholicious

Adding a Specimen

Tap the Add button at the bottom of the app. Enter a name, type (rock, mineral, fossil, gemstone, or meteorite), and any details you want to save, including location, storage spot, dimensions, weight, and a photo.

The free plan supports up to 15 specimens. Litholicious Pro removes this limit with a 7-day free trial.

Editing & Viewing Details

Tap any specimen to open its detail page. Use Edit to update information, change the photo, or mark a specimen as a favorite using the heart toggle.

Favorites can be filtered using the Favorites Only option in the Filters menu.

Filtering, Sorting & Export

On the Collection screen, use the search bar to find specimens by name, location, or category.

The Filters button allows you to filter by type, location, or favorites and sort your collection by A→Z, newest, type, or ID.

You can export your current filtered collection as a PDF using the Export PDF button (available in the live app).

Switching Views (Grid / List / Museum)

The view switcher above your collection allows you to choose between:

  • Grid: Displays specimens in a grid layout.
  • List: Displays specimens in a list format.
  • Museum: Arranges specimens into display-style cases grouped by type.

Wishlist & Saved Places

The Wishlist screen contains two sections:

  • Wish List: Specimens you want to collect.
  • Places: Locations you want to visit.

Add a wish using the + button and choose an icon or photo. In Places, add a location by name and Litholicious will place it on the map. You can mark places as visited or delete them at any time.

The Map

The Map tab displays saved specimen locations. Tap a pin to view details. Locations are created from the text you enter, so including a specific place name will provide the best results.

Litholicious Pro

Go to your Profile to manage or upgrade your plan.

Litholicious Pro provides unlimited specimens and can be canceled at any time. Your collection remains viewable after cancellation, and you can continue editing until you exceed the free limit.

Theme, Signing Out & Deleting Your Account

The Profile page allows you to:

  • Switch between light and dark mode.
  • Sign out of your account.
  • Delete your account.

Deleting your account removes your specimens, wishlist, and saved places immediately. Complete account removal finishes within 24 hours and cannot be undone.
