Litholicious Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 3, 2026

Overview

Litholicious is a personal collection app that lets you catalog your rocks, minerals, fossils, and other geological specimens, keep a wishlist, save places to visit, and store your own virtual museum.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Your name, email address, and optional profile photo used to identify you within the app.
  • Collection Content: Specimen records, notes, photographs, and other information you create and upload.
  • Wishlist & Saved Places: Wishlist entries and saved collecting locations, including location text that is converted into map coordinates.
  • Photos & Files: Images you upload, including specimen photographs and profile pictures, which are securely stored.
  • Subscription Information: Subscription status and billing period. Payment processing is handled by Stripe, which securely stores your payment information.
  • Usage Information: Limited analytics used to improve app performance, reliability, and features.

How We Use Your Information

  • Provide, synchronize, and display your collection across your devices.
  • Enable map functionality by converting saved location text into coordinates.
  • Manage subscriptions, including free trials and renewals through Stripe.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important account or policy updates.
  • Improve app performance, fix bugs, and prevent misuse.

Data Storage & Security

Your collection data is associated with your account and is visible only to you. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. However, no online system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Payment information is processed and stored by Stripe and is never stored by Litholicious.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

Information is shared only with service providers necessary to operate the app, including Stripe for payment processing and our hosting and storage providers. Information may also be disclosed if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

Your Choices

  • Edit or delete specimens, wishlist items, and saved places at any time.
  • Delete your account from the Profile page. Account deletion begins immediately, with complete removal typically occurring within 24 hours.
  • Cancel a Pro subscription at any time. Your existing data remains accessible, but new entries may be limited if you exceed the free tier limits.

Children's Privacy

Litholicious is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so it can be removed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the app after changes become effective constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
